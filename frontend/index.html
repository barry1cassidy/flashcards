Zipdeck — Flashcards for language learning and exam study

Zipdeck at zipdeck.app turns any pair of things — a word and its translation, a term and its definition — into a flashcard deck you can flip, quiz, write, match, and listen through until it sticks. Built for Chinese and Spanish lessons, MCAT and bar prep, and anything else you’re learning.

Study modes

Flip to check yourself. Quiz to recognize. Write to produce. Match to move fast. Listen when your eyes are busy. Spaced repetition times every review for faster recall.

Create with AI

Pro turns notes, a wedding toast, a keynote, or a script into a deck you can edit and rehearse — including speech, line-learning, and cloze presets.
